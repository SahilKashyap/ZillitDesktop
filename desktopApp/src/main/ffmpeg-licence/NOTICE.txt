Zillit Desktop — FFmpeg notice
==============================

Zillit Desktop uses FFmpeg to compress large videos before they are sent.
FFmpeg runs as a separate program; it is not part of Zillit's own code.

This copy is the LGPL build (no GPL-only components). It was configured with
--enable-version3 and includes these libraries, each under its own licence:
libmp3lame (LGPL), libspeex (BSD), opencore-amr and vo-amrwbenc (Apache 2.0),
OpenSSL (Apache 2.0), OpenH264 (BSD), libvpx (BSD), FreeType (FTL),
libopus (BSD), libxml2 (MIT), libsrt (MPL 2.0), libwebp (BSD), libaom (BSD),
SVT-AV1 (BSD), zimg (WTFPL).

-----------

  FFmpeg 7.1.1:     https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.1.tar.xz

Replacing FFmpeg
----------------
You may replace the FFmpeg program and libraries in this folder with your own
build of FFmpeg; Zillit Desktop will use whatever `ffmpeg` it finds here.
